## A.5.34 Privacy and Protection of PII

### Control Objective
Ensure protection of personally identifiable information (PII) in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, and contractual requirements.

### Verification Criteria
☐ PII is identified and inventory is maintained  
☐ Legal and regulatory requirements for PII protection are identified (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)  
☐ Privacy policy is established and communicated  
☐ Roles and responsibilities for PII protection are defined  
☐ Technical and organizational measures protect PII  
☐ Data subject rights are supported (access, rectification, erasure, portability)  
☐ Consent mechanisms are implemented where required  
☐ PII processing is documented (purpose, legal basis, retention)  
☐ Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA) conducted for high-risk processing  
☐ PII breach notification procedures are established

### GDPR/Privacy Compliance Checks

☐ Data minimization principle applied  
☐ Purpose limitation enforced  
☐ Storage limitation (retention periods defined and enforced)  
☐ Integrity and confidentiality measures implemented  
☐ Accountability demonstrated through documentation  

### Data Subject Rights Implementation
☐ **Right to access:** Process for customers to request their data  
☐ **Right to rectification:** Process to correct inaccurate data  
☐ **Right to erasure:** Process to delete data when required  
☐ **Right to data portability:** Process to export data in machine-readable format  
☐ **Right to object:** Process to object to certain processing  
☐ Response timeline: ≤30 days (GDPR requirement)  

### IoT-Specific PII Considerations
☐ IoT devices collecting PII are identified (cameras, biometric sensors, etc.)  
☐ Privacy notices provided at point of PII collection  
☐ Purpose of IoT data collection is transparent to data subjects  
☐ IoT data retention aligned with defined purposes  
☐ Secure deletion of PII from IoT devices when decommissioned  

### Breach Notification Readiness
☐ Breach detection capabilities in place  
☐ Breach assessment procedure defined (severity, scope, data subjects affected)  
☐ Notification to supervisory authority within 72 hours (GDPR)  
☐ Notification to affected data subjects when required  
☐ Breach documentation and records maintained
