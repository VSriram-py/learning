## USAGE INSTRUCTIONS

### How to Use These Templates

**Step 1: Determine Applicability**
- Review your Statement of Applicability (SOA)
- Identify which Annex A controls apply
- Determine relevant sector(s) from Annex D

**Step 2: Conduct Verification**
- Use templates systematically for each annex
- Gather evidence for each control/objective
- Interview responsible personnel
- Review documentation
- Test system implementations

**Step 3: Document Findings**
- Mark status for each item (✅ 🔶 ❌ N/A)
- Record specific evidence references
- Note gaps and deviations
- Assign priority levels

**Step 4: Create Action Plan**
- Prioritize findings by risk and effort
- Assign owners and due dates
- Track remediation progress
- Schedule follow-up verification

**Step 5: Report and Sign-Off**
- Compile findings into report
- Present to management
- Obtain approval to proceed
- Use for internal audit or certification preparation
